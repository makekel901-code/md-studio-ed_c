Alpha10 — current source update:
- app/src/main/java/com/amd/mdstudio/local/ProjectDiagnostics.kt: read-only structural project checks; does not claim compilation success.
- app/src/main/java/com/amd/mdstudio/local/ProjectRepairEngine.kt: targeted, user-approved AI repair drafts for existing failure-related files, with checkpoint and rollback.
- ProjectArchiveManager.kt: safe handoff copy from AI file workspace to active Gradle build project.
- MainActivity.kt: active-project local build, toolchain status, ZIP diagnostics, AI repair review/apply/rollback, and explicit assistant-to-builder handoff.
- app/build.gradle.kts: versionCode 10, versionName 1.0.0-alpha10.
- Local source repair tools are not claimed: local diagnostics are read-only and Gradle is the local compile/build tool. AI repair is offered only after a real Gradle compilation failure, not when the toolchain itself is missing or incompatible.
